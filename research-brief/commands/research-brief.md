Use the **research-brief** skill to answer the question from the sources that own
the answer.

Question:
- Use `$ARGUMENTS` when it is non-empty.
- Otherwise use the question already under discussion.

Open every source rather than citing a search snippet. Tier each one, and never
state a community claim as fact — use it as a lead to the first-party source, or
label it unverified. Pin every claim to the version or date it was verified
against. Keep both sides of a contradiction with the resolution and its reason,
and keep the open-questions section even when it is empty.
